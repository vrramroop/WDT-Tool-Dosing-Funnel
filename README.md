<h1># 3D Printed WDT-Tool-Dosing-Funnel</h1>

<h2>A tool that allows for even distribution of the coffee grounds before tamping when making espresso and a tool that allows for less spill of coffee when prepping the shot.</h2>

<h2>DISCLAIMER: 3d printing items that touch food for consumption vastly increases the risk of ingesting microplastics as well as the risk of bacteria buildup in between the layer lines. Caution is advised. Wash after every use.</h2>
